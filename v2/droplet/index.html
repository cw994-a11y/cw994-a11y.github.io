Light pours to the lowest edge, the way water settles in a glass.

02

Turn the dial.
Set today's water.

03

Use water.
Watch it drain.

In the handwashing scenario the level falls as the tap runs.

Design logic
From tilt to water.

One MPU6050 motion sensor reads which way the object leans. That lean becomes gravity for 512 cells of virtual water. Try it: drag across the panel.

ax +0.00 gay +0.00 g
Drag to tilt

Sense

The MPU6050 reads acceleration on X, Y and Z.

Map

X and Y become a direction of gravity. More lean, faster flow.

Flow

Each cell hands water to its neighbour, downhill first.

Show

The grid lights two 16 × 16 NeoPixel panels.

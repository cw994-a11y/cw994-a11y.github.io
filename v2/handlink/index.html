Function
Hold, push, pinch.

01

Hold the real object.
Its twin moves in VR.

MR-assisted calibration registers a 3D-printed prop to its virtual twin, so physical and virtual objects share one position.

02

Push with your hand.
No controller.

A physics-based virtual hand models contact, so pushing the twin behaves like pushing the real object.

03

Pinch to pick up.
Toward full grasping.

Pinch grasps pick up and place small parts, with mass and gravity modeled on the twin. Whole-hand grasping is in development.

Design logic
From hand to object.

The headset already tracks the hands, so props need no electronics. Tracked joints drive a physics hand, and its contacts move the virtual twin. Move across the panel to try it.

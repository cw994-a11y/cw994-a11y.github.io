LooMi

A little light for staying close to someone far away. Light and short words say what a text can't.

FunctionForm

Function
Light, words, one button.

01

Send a feeling.
In light.

Breathing, rainbow or a single colour. Three ways to say "I'm here" without words.

02

Say it in words.
On a tiny screen.

Short messages like "I Miss U" show on the OLED for a more personal voice.

03

One button.
Two gestures.

One press changes the light. Two quick presses switch between light and words.

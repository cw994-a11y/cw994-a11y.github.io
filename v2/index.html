Chunho Wong Mixed reality & HCI

I am a Design + Environmental Analysis student at Cornell University. I design and study mixed-reality systems that connect physical and virtual space: how people move between realities, how real objects can carry virtual interaction, and how people behave inside immersive environments.

My work combines XR development in Unity, physical prototyping and controlled human-subject studies, across Cornell's DUET Lab, the Lab of Ornithology and the Meta Design & Technology Lab.

Research experience 2024 — present

Human-subject studies and immersive environments built for them, across four Cornell labs.

  • Senior Honors Thesis

    DUET Lab, Cornell University
    Advisor: Prof. So-Yeon Yoon

    Jan 2026 — Present

    Virtual Shopping Representation and Consumer Behavior: Comparing Desktop Avatar-Based and Immersive Hand-Tracking Environments

    An independent controlled study of how interface embodiment — desktop avatars versus immersive hand tracking — shapes product-interaction behavior and purchase intention.

    Defined the comparative protocol, standardized store layout, products and tasks across conditions, and built both conditions: a desktop avatar environment in Spatial.io and a hand-tracking environment in Unity for PICO 4 Ultra.

  • Cornell Lab of Ornithology

    Experiential Learning Award recipient and Undergraduate Researcher
    Lead Mentor: Mya Thompson

    May 2026 — Present

    Built the urban comparison condition for a Cornell PhD student's international study of natural versus urban interactive 3D environments for pediatric cancer patients during chemotherapy, with Botucatu Medical School (UNESP, Brazil), Cornell's Virtual Embodiment Lab and the Lab of Ornithology.

    Translated the nature environment into a matched Unity city with aligned scale, pathways and four zones, plus first-person navigation, object-triggered video, a real-time map and Brazilian Portuguese localization.

  • Human Resonance Project

    Meta Design & Technology Lab, Research Assistant
    Faculty Supervisor: Prof. Jay Yoon

    Aug 2026 — Present

    Designed and built an interactive campfire installation for Cornell's Jill Stuart Gallery, from concept to fabrication. Ultrasonic misters, fan-driven airflow and dynamic lighting under Arduino control create the flame; the structure is 3D printed and laser cut. Iterated weekly with the PhD project lead.

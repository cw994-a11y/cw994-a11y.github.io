Research direction

Virtual–physical prototyping

How does interacting through an object, an avatar, or one's hands in VR shape the experience?

My honors thesis at the DUET Lab compares avatar-based desktop shopping with immersive hand-tracking VR. I built both environments and ran a controlled study with matched products, layouts, and tasks.

Prototype in Unity and Spatial.io, or with Arduino and fabricated parts. Study with controlled tasks, behavioral data, think-aloud testing, and usability surveys. Iterate: LooMi went through three prototype generations and evaluation with five participants.

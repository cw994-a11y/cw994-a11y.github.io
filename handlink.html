04 / Virtual–physical prototyping · Ongoing research

Sensorless
Tangible VR.

An ongoing Unity prototype for touching 3D-printed objects in immersive VR, using headset hand tracking instead of sensors on the props. MR passthrough supports initial alignment; hand colliders already push virtual objects. Natural grasping and motion estimation are the next step, with potential applications in rehabilitation, remote collaboration and training.

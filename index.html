Research direction

Virtual–physical
prototyping.

I’m interested in how an interaction’s physical or virtual form shapes what people perceive, understand and do.

For me, product design and virtual design meet in the prototype: something people can encounter, act on, and help me understand.

01 / Build

Make an interaction

Interactive objects, embodied input and simulated environments.

02 / Observe

Study the experience

Participant tasks, behavior, usability and personal interpretation.

03 / Refine

Let evidence shape it

Translate observations into the next version of the prototype.

A little about me

Chunho
Wong.

Cornell University
Ithaca, New York

I’m studying Design + Environmental Analysis at Cornell, with a focus on HCI and embodied interaction.

I build virtual environments in Unity and physical prototypes with Arduino and digital fabrication. I’m interested in how the form of an interaction changes what people notice, understand and do. I approach this through experimental design, participant studies and hands-on making.

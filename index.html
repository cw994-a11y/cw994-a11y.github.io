VR Interaction

  • Sensorless Tangible VR

    2026

    Independent work

    Grab a real object on your desk and its virtual copy in VR moves with it, with no trackers or electronics on the object. After a one-time alignment, the headset’s own hand tracking drives a physics hand that holds the object.

    Unity 6.3, PICO 4 Ultra, rigidbody physics, passthrough

  • Selective Passthrough

    2026

    Independent work

    See the real objects you choose while staying in full VR. SAM 2.1 traces any object’s outline in the camera view, so it can be shown live inside the virtual world.

    Unity 6.3, PICO 4 Ultra, SAM 2.1, passthrough

  • MR to VR Portal

    2026

    Independent work

    Pinch two corners in the air to draw a window into another world, look through it, then walk through it. Several portals can show different worlds, and the same gesture opens a way back to your room.

    Unity 6.3, PICO 4 Ultra, hand tracking, stencil shader

  • Object Portals

    2026

    Independent work

    A real object, like your monitor, becomes the window between your room and a virtual world. It combines Selective Passthrough with MR to VR Portal.

    Unity 6.3, PICO 4 Ultra, SAM 2.1, passthrough

  • AI Reality Guardian

    2026

    Independent work

    Stay in full VR and still know what is around you. The AI describes the room in live text and opens a live window wherever a real object moves, or on any object you ask about.

    Unity 6.3, PICO 4 Ultra, YOLO11n, Qwen3-VL, Whisper

  • Voice to 3D

    2026

    Independent work

    Say a sentence and a 3D object appears in mixed reality, imagined from words or copied from a real object in view. Say another and it moves, spins or changes size.

    Unity 6.3, PICO 4 Ultra, Whisper, Qwen3-VL, SAM 2.1, Hunyuan3D-2, Shap-E

Product Design

  • LooMi

    Jan — May 2025

    Independent human-centered design study

    An interactive lighting device for long-distance emotional connection: light, short words on a tiny screen and one button. Refined over three iterations with five participants, reaching a System Usability Scale score of 81.5.

    Arduino, LEDs, OLED display, 3D printing, cultural probes, think-aloud testing

  • Droplet

    DEA 3308

    Team project with Jolin Li and Jordan Jennings

    A curved LED object that makes daily water use visible. A motion sensor turns the way the object leans into gravity for 512 cells of virtual water on two LED panels.

    Arduino, MPU6050, NeoPixel panels, rotary encoder, 3D printing

  • Human Resonance Campfire

    2026

    Meta Design & Technology Lab, Faculty Supervisor: Prof. Jay Yoon

    An interactive campfire installation for Cornell’s Jill Stuart Gallery, built from concept to fabrication. A fan above ultrasonic mist draws it upward into a three-dimensional flame, lit by Arduino-controlled lighting.

    Arduino, ultrasonic misters, 3D printing, laser cutting

Research experience 2024 — present

Human-subject studies and the virtual environments built for them, across four Cornell labs.

  • Senior Honors Thesis

    DUET Lab, Cornell University
    Advisor: Prof. So-Yeon Yoon

    Jan 2026 — Present

    Realistic or Surreal? How Surreal Virtual Environments Shape User Engagement and Purchase Behavior in Browser-Based Virtual Retail

    An independent within-participant experiment, grounded in the stimulus-organism-response framework, testing whether a surreal versus realistic virtual store affects user engagement and purchase behavior.

    Building matched realistic and surreal Unity WebGL versions of a retail store with identical products, layout and controls for a planned two-site study in Hong Kong and the United States; planned measures include UES-SF engagement, logged purchase behavior and linear mixed-model analyses.

  • Cornell Lab of Ornithology

    Experiential Learning Award recipient and Undergraduate Researcher
    Lead Mentor: Mya Thompson

    May 2026 — Present

    Built the urban comparison condition for a Cornell PhD student's international study of natural versus urban interactive 3D environments for pediatric cancer patients during chemotherapy, with Botucatu Medical School (UNESP, Brazil), Cornell's Virtual Embodiment Lab and the Lab of Ornithology.

    Translated the nature environment into a matched Unity city with aligned scale, pathways and four zones, plus first-person navigation, object-triggered video, a real-time map and Brazilian Portuguese localization.

    Meta Design & Technology Lab, Research Assistant

Ultrasonic misters, fan-driven airflow and dynamic lighting under Arduino control create the flame; the structure is 3D printed and laser cut. Iterated weekly with the PhD project lead.

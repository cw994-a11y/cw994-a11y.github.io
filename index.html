Research direction

Virtual–physical prototyping

How does interacting through an object, an avatar, or one's hands in VR shape the experience?

My honors thesis at the DUET Lab compares avatar-based desktop shopping with immersive hand-tracking VR. I built both environments and designed a controlled study with matched products, layouts, and tasks.

  1. 01

    Prototype

    Build interactions in Unity and Spatial.io, or with Arduino and fabricated parts.

  2. 02

    Study

    Use controlled tasks, behavioral data, think-aloud testing, and usability surveys.

  3. 03

    Iterate

    LooMi went through three prototype generations and evaluation with five participants.

About

Designer. Maker. Researcher.

I'm Chunho, a Cornell student working across product design, VR, and HCI.

My work spans wiring sensors, fabricating enclosures, developing hand-tracking interactions, and studying use. I also teach digital fabrication as a student technician at Cornell.
